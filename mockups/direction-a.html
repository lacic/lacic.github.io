Team AI Research (AIR) about the team →

AIR is the R&D team inside Infobip. We work on applied AI for messaging, voice and customer engagement — at the scale of a platform that carries a large share of the world's business communication.

The interesting part is not the research. It is the distance between a result that holds in a paper and one that holds in production, on real traffic, for real customers. That distance is what the team exists to close.
